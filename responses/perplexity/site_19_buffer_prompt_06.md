YES.

Page: https://buffer.com/

“Connected to every platform and tool you use.”

“Create”
“Publish”
“Insights”
“Community”

“Plan and schedule your content across social media platforms”

“Understand your performance and what to post next”

(e) Anything confusing or missing for a first-time visitor?
1. The core product is clearly identified, but the homepage contains multiple audiences

The homepage presents:

“Creators”

“Small Business”

“Agencies”

“Developers”

“Higher Education”

“Nonprofits”

“Startups” (buffer.com)

This is directly observable, but the site does not state that having multiple audience categories causes confusion or user drop-off.

Therefore:

Actual first-time-user confusion/bounce: NOT FOUND on site.

2. Pricing is directly accessible

The main navigation includes:

“Pricing” (buffer.com)
