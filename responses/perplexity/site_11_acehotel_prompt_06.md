(e) Anything confusing or missing that could make a first-time visitor bounce?
1. The homepage contains a large amount of event content

The “Goings on” section begins with:

“Show Me What’s Going On”

and then presents a long sequence of dated events, including:

“Sunday Live”

“YOGA | Maria Archontaki from Hobnob”

“SOUND HEALING | Andreas Yeravshek x Hobnob”

“Poolside with Mister Enarto”

“Sound Waves Summer DJ Series”

“Mic Drop Karaoke”

and many others.

This is directly on the homepage and occupies a substantial portion of the accessible content.

2. Some event data contains inconsistent dates/text

For example, the homepage displays:

“Let’s Bingo Again Tuesdays 7-10 pm”

with:

“Ace Hotel Palm Springs 07:00 PM Tuesday July 28, 2026”

but its description contains:

“this Tuesday 12.27.25”

Those two dates are different.

Another event displays:

“Sound Waves Summer DJ Series”

with:

“Ace Hotel Palm Springs 12:00 PM Sunday July 26, 2026”

while its description refers to:

“Friday, June 5”

and a:

“JUNE DJ SCHEDULE”

These inconsistencies are present in the homepage content itself.

3. The homepage does not expose hotel-specific room information immediately

The first-screen booking interface asks for:

“Location”
“Check In”
“Check Out”
“Guests”

but does not display room types or room prices before the visitor uses the booking mechanism.

This is an observation of the accessible homepage content; there is no site statement saying why this is the case.
