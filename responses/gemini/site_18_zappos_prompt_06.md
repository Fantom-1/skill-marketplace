The homepage has extensive shopping navigation, while the company section is confined to the lower-page footer:

“### About Zappos”

“About”

“Careers”

There is no visible “Culture” section in the homepage content.

Evidence-based finding: Someone looking specifically for Zappos' culture cannot reach a section labelled “Culture” directly from the homepage based on the inspected links.
