(a) What Ace Hotel does

Homepage: https://www.acehotel.com/

The site explicitly describes the company as:

“Ace Hotel is a collection of international lifestyle hotels.”

It states that Ace Hotel operates in:

“Seattle, Palm Springs, New York, Brooklyn, Kyoto, Sydney, Toronto and Athens.”

The homepage also describes the hotels' approach:

“Our taste tends toward historical reverence hand-in-hand with considered, contemporary design”

and:

“we think really good restaurants, public art and lobby parties are important.”

It further states:

“Each hotel is globally minded but locally focused, paying tribute to the places and communities that bring them to life.”
